Explore Molly Tea Toppings

Toppings can add different textures, flavors and experiences to milk tea and other drinks. Available choices depend on the specific drink and store.

Important: The topping examples below explain common bubble tea topping types. They are not a confirmed list of current Molly Tea offerings. Check your local store for its actual selection.
COMMON TOPPING TYPES

Understand Different Textures

These examples can help you understand common bubble tea topping styles.

⚫

Tapioca Pearls

Soft, chewy pearls commonly added to milk tea. Their texture is one of the best-known features of bubble tea.

🍮

Pudding

A soft topping that can add a smooth, custard-like texture to a drink.

☁️

Cream Toppings

Cream-based additions can create a rich, smooth layer and a different drinking experience.

🍑

Fruit Additions

Fruit pieces or fruit-based additions can bring extra flavor and texture to selected drinks.

🧊

Jelly Toppings

Jelly-style toppings are often used to add a soft, bouncy or refreshing texture.

🥥

Other Additions

Some bubble tea shops offer additional ingredients. Ask the store about its current options.

CUSTOMIZATION GUIDE

How to Choose Your Toppings

Keep these simple points in mind when ordering.

01

Choose Your Drink

Start with a milk tea, matcha, fruit tea or another available drink.

02

Think About Texture

Decide whether you prefer chewy, soft, creamy or fruity additions.

03

Confirm Availability

Ask about topping compatibility, additional charges and ingredient information.

BEFORE YOU ORDER

Things to Check

Allergens and ingredients: Some toppings may contain dairy, soy, coconut, gelatin or other ingredients. Recipes vary. Always ask the store directly about allergens and dietary requirements.
