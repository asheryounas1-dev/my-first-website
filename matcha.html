Explore Molly Tea Matcha

Matcha is a finely ground green tea traditionally associated with Japanese tea culture. Matcha-based drinks are often enjoyed for their distinctive green color and earthy flavor. Explore the current Molly Tea menu to see which matcha selections are available at your local store.

Menu note: Specific matcha drinks, ingredients and availability can vary by location. This guide does not guarantee that every drink is currently available.
